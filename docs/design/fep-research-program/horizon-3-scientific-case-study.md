## Native inference, control and information claims

The finite-latent experiment fixes one latent value and draws independent noisy
observations of that same value. Its uncapped posterior variance, joint MSE,
Markov tail bound and limit establish joint-law convergence in probability.
They do not establish fixed-truth or almost-sure consistency without a further
argument. Sequential OU filtering is a different experiment.

Hidden-mode nonidentifiability uses distinct initial full-state laws with equal
all-finite projected path laws and noisy-observation joint laws. Equality of one
time marginal alone is insufficient. Scalar mean identification applies only
to its specified observation experiment.

The two-step controller uses one observation between full-state transitions.
Its objective is the native terminal squared scalar coordinate. The pointwise
second-action selector chooses `false` when the posterior mean is nonnegative;
first-action integrated risks tie exactly, so the frozen first action is
`false`. Native cost, exact attainment, class containment and a finite full-state
moment bound support this finite horizon. A restricted class containing only
`(true, true)` has strictly worse risk than an opposite open-loop plan.

Native Gaussian-channel mutual information is derived from the actual joint and
product laws, Radon–Nikodym density and integrability. The centered-preference
EFE/risk ordering has explicit premises; a shifted-preference counterexample
chooses a different action. These results do not equate every EFE objective
with quadratic risk or prove an infinite-horizon controller.

The nonstationary three-time uncontrolled experiment uses the actual forward and
reversed joint path laws. Both directions have mutual absolute continuity and
integrable log ratios on the positive grid, with positive finite KL. A repeated
`[0, 0, 1/4]` grid has a support mismatch and infinite KL; the all-zero grid is a
separate zero-KL control. A fixed-center marginal KL equals `exp(−4t)` and is a
Lyapunov witness for that experiment, not a monotonicity result under switched
actions. Hypothetical energy-per-nat factors `1` and `2` map the same positive
KL to different quantities, so no measured heat or physical entropy production
is identified without constitutive measurements.
