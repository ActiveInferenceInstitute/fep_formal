## Scientific firewalls that survive the cleanup

- H1's repaired terminal theorem uses one finite posterior–decision–action
  carrier with exact generative transition/semigroup bridges. Its original
  uninhabited first merge remains a countermodel. A one-step posterior decision
  does not become transition-aware or EFE-optimal planning.
- H2's scalar Gaussian learning, latent-state filter, one-step quadratic control
  and finite-grid path lanes are distinct. The selected Boolean mean
  parameter's fixed-truth consistency is not a theorem about H3's continuous
  latent experiment.
- The exact H2 four-coordinate symmetric precision, its derived inverse
  covariance and native conditioning seam preceded H3. H3 reuses that carrier;
  it does not substitute another state, reset hidden modes or assume its
  scientific conclusions in model fields.
- Conditional-independence and posterior identities are on their named laws
  and support, often almost everywhere. Stationarity, precision zeros and
  blanket factorization do not identify an intervention or a biological causal
  mechanism.
- Native extended KL and totalized real finite KL have explicit support
  boundaries. Finite-grid KL does not construct an SDE or continuous-path law.
  A Lyapunov or information identity does not identify physical heat.
- The frozen H3 synthetic result can establish implementation recovery and
  calibration on generated data only. Empirical, causal, organism/cognition
  and universal FEP claims remain unavailable without their own evidence.
- Optional manifold packaging, generator/path lifting and broader control
  remain prospective scopes with independent stop/go probes. They cannot
  replace a missing current-cycle acceptance gate.
