# H2.3a: selected Gaussian posterior martingale

Status: **accepted; downstream H2.3b has also exited**.

## Outcome

Construct one static two-mean Gaussian parameter-learning model and prove that
the probability assigned by Mathlib's native finite-prefix posterior to the
`true` mean is almost everywhere equal to a conditional-expectation
martingale. Prove its almost-everywhere Lévy upward limit to the conditional
expectation under the sigma-algebra generated by all finite observations.
This slice does not identify that limit with the
hidden parameter and does not call the result posterior consistency.

The probability measure for the martingale and limit is the selected
prior-predictive joint law on the static hypothesis and its infinite
observation trajectory. It is not either truth-conditional trajectory law.

## Frozen model and carriers

The selected model has variance `1`, means `false ↦ 0` and `true ↦ 1`, and
the fair H1.3 prior embedded as a native measure. The hypothesis is sampled
once. Conditional on that static hypothesis, all real-valued observations are
i.i.d. from the corresponding H2.1 `FixedVarianceGaussian.law`.

Concretely, `selectedObservationLaw hypothesis` is
`selectedGaussianFamily.law (selectedMean hypothesis)`, and
`selectedObservationKernel` is `Kernel.boolKernel` with the false law first
and the true law second. `selectedMeanPrior` is
`FEP.NativeBlanket.embeddedLaw FEP.FinitePosteriorLearning.selectedPrior`.
`selectedTrajectoryLaw hypothesis` is `Measure.infinitePi` of the constant
coordinate law, `selectedTrajectoryKernel` is the corresponding Boolean
kernel, and `selectedJointLaw` is the native prior--trajectory `compProd`.

The central bridge is measure-scoped:

```lean
posteriorProbability n =ᵐ[selectedJointLaw]
  selectedJointLaw[
    selectedParameterIndicator | observationFiltration n]
```

`posteriorProbability_stronglyAdapted` is genuine strong adaptedness, not only
almost-everywhere measurability. `posteriorProbability_integrable n` is under
`selectedJointLaw`. `posteriorProbability_mem_Icc` may be pointwise because
native posterior rows are probability measures; no pointwise posterior--
conditional-expectation equality follows from it.

The martingale theorem is exactly about `posteriorProbability`,
`observationFiltration`, and `selectedJointLaw`. The limit theorem states
that, for `selectedJointLaw`-almost every sample, the sequence
`fun n => posteriorProbability n sample` tends along `Filter.atTop` to
`posteriorLimit sample`. It must use root
`MeasureTheory.tendsto_ae_condExp`, whose endpoint is the conditional
expectation under `⨆ n, observationFiltration n`.

Do not use `Integrable.tendsto_ae_condExp` with a premise asserting that the
hidden-parameter indicator is measurable under the limiting observation
sigma-algebra `⨆ n, observationFiltration n`. That premise is the
identification step reserved for H2.3b. This increasing union is not the
Kolmogorov tail sigma-field.

`finitePosterior_eventualContraction_regression` is a same-signature wrapper
around
`FEP.FinitePosteriorLearning.posteriorBadMass_eventually_contracts`. Its
almost-everywhere measure remains H1.3's Boolean `trajectoryLaw`; it proves no
equality between the Boolean and Gaussian observation or trajectory carriers.

## Proof ladder and first stop/go

1. Instantiate the variance-one, zero/one-mean H2.1 family and native
   observation kernel.
2. Construct the conditional infinite-product trajectory laws, fair native
   prior, trajectory kernel, and prior-predictive joint law.
3. Construct the natural observation filtration and finite-prefix maps; prove
   the product-coordinate and finite-prefix laws.
4. Map the selected joint law to parameter--prefix pairs and use the existing
   posterior reconstruction law plus Mathlib conditional-distribution
   uniqueness.
5. **First hard stop/go:** prove
   `posteriorProbability_ae_eq_condExp` for the actual native finite-prefix
   posterior. If this theorem does not compile, stop H2.3a; an abstract
   conditional-expectation process is not an acceptable substitute.
6. Derive strong adaptedness, integrability, bounds, and martingale status via
   `martingale_condExp` and `Martingale.congr`.
7. Apply Lévy upward convergence, combine the countable per-time a.e.
   equalities, and transfer the pointwise `Filter.atTop` limit.
8. Add the exact H1.3 regression wrapper without a carrier bridge.

Expected elaboration pressure is concentrated at `Set.Iic` versus
`Finset.Iic`, `Kernel.boolKernel` false/true orientation, standard-Borel and
probability instances for product carriers, `compProd`/map/swap orientation,
pulling prefix-predictive a.e. equalities back to the joint law, ENNReal-to-real
kernel mass, and `ae_all_iff` when transferring the limit.

## Scientific exclusions and no-go

This slice is static two-point parameter learning with Gaussian observations.
It is not latent-state filtering, a continuous-parameter conjugate Gaussian
posterior, an OU transition, a path-law theorem, weak posterior convergence,
decision-risk convergence, a contraction rate, or transfer of entropy, log
loss, or another unbounded observable.

The posterior martingale holds under `selectedJointLaw`; no martingale claim is
made under either fixed-truth trajectory law. Native posterior and conditional
distribution versions are semantically connected only almost everywhere
under the named predictive/joint measure. Almost-everywhere equality is never
promoted to pointwise equality.

If the concrete native posterior bridge, the prior-predictive joint model, the
observation filtration, or Lévy upward limiting-observation endpoint fails, H2.3a is blocked
and H2.3b stays closed. Retain no field or local structure that carries
martingale, convergence, identification, or consistency as supplied evidence.
