## Independent review

Three fresh reviewers examined scientific scope, Lean/API structure, and
skeptical carrier/claim boundaries independently. Scientific and Lean/API
reviews approved the unchanged proof bodies. The skeptical review initially
rejected the missing typed-consumer protection, then approved the added
consumer/negative-test design and the append-only custody validator. Native
verification followed those reviews.

The VFE KL-gap identity is definitional; Gaussian KL evaluation, uniqueness,
derivative and metric duality supply the substantive results. Recognition
variance is fixed. Descent is only at time zero along a frozen-direction line.
Native posterior agreement is evidence-almost-everywhere, including when a
closed version is evaluated at zero. Static parameter learning and latent-state
filtering retain different posterior carriers. Control is a one-step squared-
risk comparison, not EFE optimality. Fin4 is separate from the scalar model;
its non-independence diagnostic uses a separate fixed Fin2 model.
