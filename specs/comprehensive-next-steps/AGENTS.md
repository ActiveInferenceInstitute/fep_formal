# Comprehensive continuation contract

Follow the [repository contract](../../AGENTS.md), [spec contract](../AGENTS.md)
and [research contract](../../docs/design/fep-research-program/research-contract.md).

- README.md owns the current pickup and order; work-packages.json owns explicit
  package contracts, not catalogue or scientific claims.
- Preserve immutable freezes, evidence, primary seeds and published signatures.
- Source reviews are feasibility evidence until their compiling or execution
  probes actually pass.
- Reuse existing production owners. A new owner/resource requires its declared
  provenance and namespace review.
- choices.md records decisions before their consequences spread across passes.
