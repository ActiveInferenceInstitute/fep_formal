# FEP Formal: improvement scope, 2026-10-08

This review scopes new work across the package infrastructure, tooling, docs,
the Lean formal layer, and the formalism catalogue itself. It complements the
canonical open backlog in [TODO.md](../../TODO.md) and its linked Issues; nothing
here duplicates an open row. Where a candidate builds on one, it says
"extends #N". Every open candidate is filed as an Issue
([#84](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/fep_formal/issues/84)–[#146](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/fep_formal/issues/146)) with owner, scope, dependencies and
acceptance, and listed in [TODO.md](../../TODO.md); the Issue, not this snapshot, is
the current record.

Sizes follow the 2026-09-30 convention: **minor** is an owner-local change;
**medium** crosses a package, workflow or mathematical boundary; **major**
needs staged evidence and independent acceptance. Sizes are scope, not
schedule. No candidate below is a claim: catalogue mode, native Lean and full
Hermes/OpenGauss remain separate evidence planes, and compilation never
promotes a semantic disposition.

## Baseline

- Reviewed at `main` = `34f8f33` (PR #82 merge), tag `v1.6.0` present.
- Python package: 117 source modules; 3,373 collected tests in 141 files;
  `fep-lean` CLI cold start ≈ 0.64 s, dominated by catalogue-registry build at
  import and module-level matplotlib.
- Lean formal layer: 71 formal modules (45 foundations, 25 composition
  leaves, one aggregate), ≈ 36.6k LOC; zero `sorry`/`admit`/`axiom`/
  `native_decide`; one `set_option maxHeartbeats` (`h3_case_study.lean:1083`).
- Catalogue: 168 topics, 22 families; dispositions 149 formalized, 13
  conditional, 6 structural. Relation graph: 146 edges (20 formal, 118 formal
  pairings, 8 conceptual); 135 topics have no derivational (`formal`) edge.
- Numerical dashboard: 17 catalogue witnesses plus 2 `horizon2`; the five
  `core-*` families (50 topics) have none.
- Counts were measured with `rg`, AST scans and the package's own APIs.
  `lake build` was not run; Mathlib-name feasibility notes are unverified
  against the pinned revision unless stated.

## Formalism catalogue

The 149 "formalized" dispositions are judged against each row's narrowed
invariant, not its title, so they are a weak signal of scientific coverage.
By area, the non-formalized share is FEP 5%, Active Inference 11%, Bayesian
Mechanics 17%, Thermodynamics 11%, Information Geometry 14%. The weakest
families are **control-and-planning-as-inference** (067, 069, 070 structural;
071 conditional), **causal-blankets-and-interventions** (079, 083, 085
conditional; 081 structural) and **path-space-stochastic-thermodynamics** (094
structural; 095, 097 conditional). All targets below feed
[FORM-D3](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/fep_formal/issues/75)
as concrete briefs; none promotes a disposition without independent review.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Cheap correctness first:** PKG-1, TOOL-3, DOC-2, LEAN-8, and the
   `lean-landscape` "155" generator fix.
2. **Formalism quick wins** that strengthen existing proxies without new
   theory: FORM-S4, FORM-S2, FORM-S8, then FORM-S1 and FORM-S3. Each
   is a FORM-D3 brief with its own independent review.
3. **Structural consolidation** in one coordinated provenance-roster refresh:
   PKG-2, PKG-5, PKG-7, PKG-13, TOOL-4, LEAN-1, LEAN-2.
4. **New theory lanes** behind library probes: FORM-S6 → S5, S7, S10, S11,
   S13, S15; then S12 stage 2 and S14/LEAN-14 only after a Mathlib gap memo.
5. **Upstreaming** (LEAN-12, LEAN-13) once the corresponding FEP-side
   statements are stable.

Before release evidence is claimed for any of these, the
[release procedure](../../docs/release.md) and the exact-current-source acceptance
rules in `TODO.md` apply unchanged.
