## Fixed acceptance criteria

1. Status never labels an unavailable comparison current; static readiness
   starts no process, acquires nothing, changes no bytes/mtimes, and cannot
   stand in for runtime publication validation.
2. Installed wheels pass real isolated core API/resource/help acceptance on
   advertised Python/platform support, with CPython 3.14 evidence validation
   separate. Unsupported Q7 interpreters reject before parsing until a reviewed
   version-stable canonical digest is proven and custody re-pinned.
3. Appropriate documentation PR gates run before merge, and accepted render
   PDF/receipt/font/renderer/source manifests are retained at the exact CI SHA.
4. One existing-owner readiness/capture model preserves strict validators,
   detects source changes, bounds/resumes stages, and produces two byte-identical
   bundles accepted by independent validation before publication.
5. Deterministic identity and asymmetric coarsening witnesses compile with
   explicit zero/reference support boundaries and honest maturity records.
6. Relative-support finite/native KL and mutual-information carriers are
   identified formally, including deterministic garbling and singular failures.
7. Actual finite rate-distortion feasibility, optimizer attainment and a
   derived dual lower bound are proved, with zero multiplier, infeasible budget
   and nonunique optimizer cases. No assumed result field or sorry substitutes.
8. H3 has independently accepted exactly-one branch, immutable pre-outcome
   protocol, manifested model and cross-domain chain, full synthetic recovery
   and negative controls, optional licensed empirical result or governed no-go,
   independent claim matrix and clean-environment bundle reproduction.
9. Declared repository checks pass in their real scope. Native compilation,
   provider, numerical, Python, browser/render and publication evidence stay
   separate. Final source bytes, receipt digests and remote identity are audited.

## Controls and stopping rules

Four dependency pins are snapshotted around setup; no pin upgrade is part of
this repair. Lean commands are serialized. Agents have disjoint source ownership.
All failed probes and protocol deviations are retained. Source/receipt mutation
invalidates dependent results; it never authorizes rewriting history as a pass.
No current-source claim is made from historical CI or receipts without validation.

A failure narrows scientific claims or closes the empirical gate according to
its preregistered rule; it does not shrink the implementation objective. Provider
execution requires an actual credential/spend boundary. New data governance,
publication or repository settings receive their required concrete review.

## Review checkpoints

Independent infrastructure review follows package changes. H3 methodology,
formal, domain and statistical reviews precede their acceptance stages; reviewers
must not author the same terminal evidence. The Science full cycle is used for
measurement and revision, not to infer physical FEP validity from compilation.
